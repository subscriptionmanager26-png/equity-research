## 1. Sector Context

India’s capital-markets ecosystem splits into three layers:

1. **Exchanges (BSE)** — volume-linked, high operating leverage, regulatory overhang on derivatives.
2. **Brokers (Groww, Angel One)** — client acquisition + monetisation; highly sensitive to F&O/cash turnover and SEBI rule changes.
3. **Depositories (CDSL)** — account-linked annuities + transaction/KYC fees; slower but more predictable.

**Macro tailwinds (Sep 2026):** Demat accounts continue to grow (~1 lakh/day for CDSL in Q2 FY27E per HSIE); mainboard IPO pipeline re-accelerated (50 IPOs in Q2 FY27 vs 9 in Q1). **Headwinds:** F&O participation normalisation after 2025 regulatory tightening; geopolitical risk episodes compress retail activity (visible in Angel One’s FY26 PAT decline).

### 3.2 Groww / Billionbrains Garage Ventures (NSE: GROWW)
